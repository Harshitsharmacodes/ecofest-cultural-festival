Analytical Challenge: How Overlapping Time Slots Remain Readable

In college festivals, exciting events often happen simultaneously (e.g. Nature Photo Walk and Eco-Clay Sculpting both start at 10:00 AM on Day 2). To prevent visual chaos, our schedule uses an Adaptive Multi-Track Venue Matrix:

  • Distinct Venue Lanes: Concurrent events never overwrite or stack on top of each other; instead, they render in side-by-side proportional sub-columns partitioned by venue zones.
  • Distinct Color Chips & Icons: Each venue (Whispering Banyan, Emerald Amphitheatre, Moss Lab, Sunshine Grove) carries a persistent color token so attendees can visually parse multiple parallel tracks at a glance.
  • Synchronized Time Pillars: An explicit left-hand timeline anchor establishes chronological progression, giving clear visual boundaries for start and end times.

Campus Guide & Sacred Zones

Find your way around the enchanted grounds of Greenwood Campus.

Zone A

The Whispering Banyan

Main inaugural stage sheltered beneath the 120-year-old banyan canopy. Hosted acoustic concerts, keynote ceremonies, and cultural dramas.

💧 Reusable Water Dispenser 🔋 Solar Charging Pod
Zone B

Emerald Amphitheatre

Terraced grass seating with natural acoustic reflections. Home to poetry recitals, fashion walks from recycled fabrics, and evening firefly dances.

🪑 Grass Terraces ♿ Wheelchair Ramp Access
Zone C

Moss & Bio-Mineral Lab

Indoor-outdoor hybrid studio. Contains soil testing benches, pottery wheels, seedling incubators, and macro photography darkrooms.

🔬 Bio-Tools Station 🧼 Eco-Washing Sink
Zone D

Sunshine Grove & Nursery

The open meadow for hands-on plantation, seed-bombing tournaments, organic food stalls, and upcycled art installations.
